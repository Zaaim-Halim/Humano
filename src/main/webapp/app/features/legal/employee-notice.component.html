Privacy notice for employees of [Employer name]

[Employer legal name], [registered address] (“we”), is responsible, as the controller, for the personal data we hold about you as a current, former or prospective employee or worker. This notice explains what we hold, why, and your rights. Questions: [HR / privacy contact email][, our data protection officer: contact details].

What we hold

  • Identity and contact details: name, photo, date of birth, gender, marital status, nationality, addresses, email, phone.
  • Government identifiers: national ID, passport, social security and tax identification numbers.
  • Your employment: job, position, department, manager, contracts, start and end dates, working time, education, training and licences.
  • Pay: salary and other compensation, deductions, tax, payslips, and the bank account (IBAN) we pay you into.
  • Time and absence: leave requests and balances, timesheets.
  • Performance: goals, reviews, feedback, ratings and improvement plans.
  • Emergency contacts you give us.
  • Health-related data, only where needed: [for example a medical profile (blood type, allergies, emergency medical notes), health insurance details, reasons for sick leave].
  • Documents you or we upload, and records of your use of the HR system (sign-ins, changes made).

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To manage your employment and pay you — necessary for your employment contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To meet our legal obligations: tax and social-security reporting, payroll records, working-time and employment law (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To run the organisation: reporting lines, performance management, planning and security of our systems — our legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f)), which you may object to.
  • Health data and other special categories only where employment, social-security or social-protection law requires or permits it (art. 9(2)(b)), or to protect your vital interests in an emergency (art. 9(2)(c)).

Who sees it

  • Within [Employer name]: HR and payroll staff, and your managers for what their role needs — access is controlled by role.
  • {{ legal.product }}, the HR and payroll software we use, which stores and processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement, and its own service providers (hosting, email delivery, payments).
  • Tax, social-security and other authorities, our bank for salary payments, [pension / insurance providers], as the law requires.
  • [Our accountants, auditors, legal advisers.]

[Where data is transferred outside your country / the EEA, describe the safeguards, e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses.]

How long we keep it

For the duration of your employment and afterwards for as long as the law requires or allows: [for example payroll and tax records for N years, performance reviews for N years after the review period, other records N years after you leave]. We then delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to see the data we hold about you and get a copy, have it corrected or completed, have it erased or its use restricted where the law allows, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Contact [HR / privacy contact email]. You may also complain to the data protection authority: [name and website of the authority].
