1. Who is responsible for your data

{{ legal.product }} plays two different roles, depending on whose data it is:

  • We are the controller of the personal data of visitors to our website, people who request a demo, the people who sign up for and administer a workspace, billing contacts, and anyone who contacts us. This policy covers that data.

If you are an employee and want to see, correct or delete your HR data, please contact your employer’s HR team; if you contact us, we will pass your request on to them. Employers can start from our privacy notice template to inform their staff.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party tracking scripts run on our pages. The site and app use your browser’s local and session storage only for things that are strictly necessary or that you choose:

  • Sign-in token — keeps you signed in; kept for the browser session, or longer if you choose “Remember me”.
  • Workspace — which workspace you are signing in to, for the browser session.
  • Preferences — light or dark theme, display density, language, list views and dismissed hints.

Because these are strictly necessary for the service you ask for, or only remember your own choices, they do not require consent. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings; you will then be signed out. Fonts and all other page resources are served from our own servers, so loading a page does not contact any third party.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and workspace data: for as long as the workspace exists, then as set out in our Terms (deleted within {{ legal.deletionDays }} days of the end of the subscription, and from backups within a further {{ legal.backupDays }} days).
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: nothing is stored until you confirm your email — the details you entered travel only inside the signed link we email you (apart from the email delivery itself).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased, or its use restricted, in the cases the law provides;
  • receive data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format, and have it sent to someone else (portability);
  • object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, and always to direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it, without affecting what we did before;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in particular in the country where you live or work, or where an alleged infringement took place. Our lead authority is {{ legal.supervisoryAuthority }}.

To use these rights, email {{ legal.privacyEmail }}. We will reply within one month (extendable by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you), and may need to confirm your identity first. Exercising your rights is free unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive. California residents have equivalent rights to know, delete and correct, and we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

8. Security

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including a separate database for each workspace, encrypted connections, hashed passwords, permission-based access controls and audit logging. These are described in more detail in Annex 2 of the Data Processing Addendum. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data and is likely to put your rights at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay.

9. Children

{{ legal.product }} is a business service and not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for our own purposes.
